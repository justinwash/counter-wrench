---
title: Diagnosing wobbles, flats and slow leaks

Wheel and tire problems are usually simple, but a wobble can also be a steering or frame problem, which is a safety issue.

## Wheel wobble

A wheel that wobbles side to side when spun is out of true, usually from a hit or a loose or broken spoke. Truing is `WHEEL-TRUE`; a broken spoke is `SPOKE-REPLACE`. A wheel that wobbles because the tire isn't seated evenly on the rim needs the tire reseated. A dented or cracked rim is a safety issue.

## Play in the wheel

If the wheel moves side to side at the axle when the frame is held still, the hub bearings are loose or worn (`HUB-SERVICE`), or the axle isn't tight. Check the thru-axle or quick release first; a loose one is a safety issue.

## A wobbly feel when riding

If the bike feels wobbly or vague when riding but the wheels spin true, check the headset for play: hold the front brake and rock the bike. Headset play is a safety issue until fixed (`HEADSET-ADJUST`). A fork or frame that's bent or cracked can also cause it; that's safety-critical, and the bike shouldn't be ridden.

## Flat tires

For a flat, find the cause before fitting a new tube, or it'll flat again. Common causes: a thorn or glass still in the tire, a pinch flat from low pressure (two small slits side by side), a rim tape that's shifted and exposed a spoke hole, or a worn-out tire. A fix with a new tube is `FLAT-REPAIR`. Repeated flats from the same spot point to the rim tape or something stuck in the tire. A cut sidewall needs a new tire (`TIRE-INSTALL`).

## Slow leaks on tubeless tires

A tubeless tire that slowly loses air over a few days usually has dried-out sealant, which needs topping up every few months (`SEALANT-REFRESH`). Other causes: a loose or clogged valve core, damaged rim tape, or a bead that isn't sealed. A full tubeless setup from scratch is `TUBELESS-SETUP`. Some slow pressure loss over a week is normal for tubeless; see the "that's normal" guide.

## Slow leaks on tubes

A tube that loses air over a day or two usually has a tiny puncture or a leaky valve. A valve core that's loose (on Presta valves with removable cores) can also leak. Most slow leaks with tubes are fixed with a new tube.
