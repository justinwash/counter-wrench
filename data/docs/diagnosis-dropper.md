---
title: Diagnosing dropper post problems
doc_type: diagnosis
complaints: [dropper_issue]
---

Dropper posts have two common problem areas: the cable and lever, which are usually an adjustment, and the post's internals, which are a service.

## Won't drop or won't return

A dropper that won't move when the lever is pressed, or won't come back up, usually has a cable that's slipped, stretched or come loose at the lever or the post. That's an adjustment or a cable replacement (`SHIFT-CABLE` covers a dropper cable and housing). Some droppers are hydraulic or wireless and are diagnosed differently.

## Slow to return

A dropper that rises slowly usually needs its cable tension checked, or on air-sprung posts, its air pressure topped up. Cold weather slows most droppers. If it's still slow after that, it needs a service (`DROPPER-SERVICE`).

## Sagging or sinking under the rider

A dropper that slowly sinks when sat on, or has vertical play at the top of its travel, usually has air mixed into its oil, which needs a service (`DROPPER-SERVICE`). Some sideways wiggle in the post head is normal for many droppers; see the "that's normal" guide.

## Creaks from the dropper

A creak when seated on a dropper bike can come from the dropper's internals or its clamp, not just the seat clamp. A dropper creak usually needs a service (`DROPPER-SERVICE`), unlike a rigid seatpost, where a clean and grease is usually enough.
